About

This is my long-term journal of places, distance and human presence.

I am drawn to quiet moments: figures seen through structures, relationships held within space, and scenes that might otherwise pass without attention.

Each entry is a carefully selected sequence of seven photographs, offering an impression of a place rather than a complete record of it.

Privacy & analytics

This journal keeps essential browser preferences locally so the website works as expected. Google Analytics is optional. If you choose “Accept analytics”, it helps me understand which pages and photo albums visitors view and which album links they open. “Reject optional” keeps analytics disabled. The journal works regardless of your decision.

If you accept, Google Analytics uses cookies to measure page visits, album-link events and browsing activity on this journal. Google also processes standard technical data for analytics, which can include approximate location and browser or device information. I do not intentionally submit names, email addresses or form contents. Google processes this information under its own terms.

Advertising or personalised marketing tracking is not currently enabled. It is not included in your analytics choice. If I introduce it later, I will describe its actual purposes and request a separate, optional choice before enabling it.

On your first visit, you can accept, reject or manage optional categories. You can revise your choice through “Privacy choices” in the footer. Rejecting analytics later stops future tracking in this browser and attempts to remove this site's Google Analytics cookies. Previously collected data is not automatically deleted. Choices are stored in your browser and are renewed after approximately six months or when the policy changes.

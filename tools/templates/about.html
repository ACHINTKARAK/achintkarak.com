About

This is my long-term journal of places, distance and human presence.

I am drawn to quiet moments: figures seen through structures, relationships held within space, and scenes that might otherwise pass without attention.

Each entry is a carefully selected sequence of seven photographs, offering an impression of a place rather than a complete record of it.

Privacy & analytics

This journal keeps essential browser preferences locally so the website can work as expected. Google Analytics is optional. If you choose “Accept analytics”, it helps me understand which pages and photo albums visitors view and which album links they open. “Only necessary” keeps analytics disabled, and you can still use the full website.

If you accept, Google may set analytics cookies and receive page-view information, album-link events and standard technical information such as device details and approximate location. I do not intentionally send names, email addresses or form contents. Analytics data is not used by this journal for advertising. Google processes analytics data under its own terms.

You can change your choice through the “Analytics preferences” control in the footer. Declining later stops future tracking and attempts to remove this site's Google Analytics cookies. Previously collected data is not automatically deleted.
